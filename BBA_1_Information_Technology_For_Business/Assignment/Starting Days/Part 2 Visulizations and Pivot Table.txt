======================================================================
CHART 5: ATTENDANCE VS PERCENTAGE
======================================================================

PURPOSE:
Study the relationship between attendance and academic performance.

STEP 1:

Select:

Attendance
and
Percent

Q1:Q10
I1:I10

STEP 2:

Insert
→ Scatter
→ Scatter with only Markers

STEP 3:

Chart title:

"Attendance vs Percentage"

STEP 4:

Add Trendline:

Right Click Data Points
→ Add Trendline
→ Linear

Tick:

"Display R-squared value on chart"


CORRELATION FORMULA:

=CORREL(Q2:Q10,I2:I10)


INTERPRETATION:

Close to +1
→ strong positive relationship

Close to 0
→ weak/no linear relationship

Close to -1
→ strong negative relationship


EXERCISE:

1. Does higher attendance appear related to higher marks?
2. What is the correlation value?
3. Is the relationship strong or weak?
4. What does the trendline show?

======================================================================
PART 8: TOP 5 STUDENTS
======================================================================

Use:

=SORT(A2:T10,8,-1)


This sorts the dataset according to:

Column 8 = Total


For the top 5:

=TAKE(SORT(A2:T10,8,-1),5)


If TAKE is not available, use:

=SORT(A2:T10,8,-1)

and manually examine the first 5 records.


EXERCISE:

Find:

1. Top 1 student
2. Top 3 students
3. Top 5 students
4. Bottom 3 students











======================================================================
PART 9: TOP STUDENT FORMULAS
======================================================================

HIGHEST TOTAL:

=MAX(H2:H10)


STUDENT WITH HIGHEST TOTAL:

=INDEX(B2:B10,MATCH(MAX(H2:H10),H2:H10,0))


HIGHEST PERCENTAGE:

=MAX(I2:I10)


STUDENT WITH HIGHEST PERCENTAGE:

=INDEX(B2:B10,MATCH(MAX(I2:I10),I2:I10,0))

1. Highest total

CHALLENGE EXERCISES
======================================================================

CHALLENGE 1:
Find students who satisfy BOTH:

Percentage >= 60
AND
Attendance >= 75


Formula:

=FILTER(A2:Y10,(I2:I10>=60)*(Q2:Q10>=75))


CHALLENGE 2:
Find students who need support because:

Percentage < 50
OR
Attendance < 60


Formula:

=FILTER(A2:Y10,(I2:I10<50)+(Q2:Q10<60))


CHALLENGE 3:
Find students with:

Percentage >= 80
AND
Study Hours >= 5


Formula:

=FILTER(A2:Y10,(I2:I10>=80)*(R2:R10>=5))


CHALLENGE 4:

Attendance and Percentage
