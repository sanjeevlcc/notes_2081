4.1 E-BUSINESS MODELS AND STRATEGIES
======================================================================

E-BUSINESS:
    E-business means using the Internet and digital technologies
    to perform and manage business activities.

    It includes:
        - Buying and selling
        - Online marketing
        - Customer service
        - Online banking
        - Supply chain management
        - Electronic communication


E-COMMERCE:
    E-commerce is mainly concerned with buying and selling
    products or services electronically.

IMPORTANT:

    E-commerce = Online buying + selling

    E-business = E-commerce + Other digital business activities





EXAMPLE:

    A customer purchases a mobile phone from an online store.

        Searching product
              ↓
        Add to cart
              ↓
        Online payment
              ↓
        Order processing
              ↓
        Delivery


This is E-COMMERCE.



If the same company also uses digital systems for:

    - Inventory management
    - Employee management
    - Supplier management
    - Customer support
    - Online marketing

Then it is part of E-BUSINESS.




======================================================================
E-BUSINESS MODEL
======================================================================

An E-business model describes HOW a business creates value,
delivers products/services and earns revenue using digital
technologies.


Simple formula:

    E-BUSINESS MODEL

        =
    Customer
        +
    Product/Service
        +
    Digital Platform
        +
    Revenue



======================================================================
MAJOR E-BUSINESS / E-COMMERCE MODELS
======================================================================

1. B2C
2. B2B
3. C2C
4. C2B
5. B2G
6. G2C


======================================================================
1. B2C — BUSINESS TO CONSUMER
======================================================================

Business sells products/services directly to individual customers.


Example:

    Online shop -> Customer


Examples:

    Customer buys shoes from an online store.


Flow:

    BUSINESS
       |
       v
    WEBSITE / APP
       |
       v
    CUSTOMER


Advantages:

    - Large customer reach
    - 24/7 availability
    - Easy online payment
    - Personalized marketing


======================================================================
2. B2B — BUSINESS TO BUSINESS
======================================================================

One business sells products/services to another business.


Example:

    Manufacturer -> Wholesaler


Another example:

    A company purchases cloud services from a technology provider.


Flow:

    BUSINESS A
         |
         v
    ONLINE PLATFORM
         |
         v
    BUSINESS B


Characteristics:

    - Large transactions
    - Long-term relationships
    - Bulk purchasing
    - Negotiated prices


======================================================================
3. C2C — CONSUMER TO CONSUMER
======================================================================

One consumer sells products/services to another consumer using
an online platform.


Example:

    Person A sells a used laptop to Person B through an
    online marketplace.


Flow:

    Consumer A
        |
        v
    Online Platform
        |
        v
    Consumer B


======================================================================
4. C2B — CONSUMER TO BUSINESS
======================================================================

Individual consumers provide products/services to businesses.


Examples:

    - Freelancer provides service to a company
    - Photographer sells photographs to a company
    - Influencer promotes a company's product


Flow:

    CONSUMER
       |
       v
    SERVICE / PRODUCT
       |
       v
    BUSINESS


======================================================================
5. B2G — BUSINESS TO GOVERNMENT
======================================================================

Businesses provide products/services to government organizations
through digital systems.


Example:

    Software company
          |
          v
    Government organization


Examples:

    - Government software contracts
    - Online procurement
    - IT services


======================================================================
6. G2C — GOVERNMENT TO CITIZEN
======================================================================

Government provides digital services to citizens.


Examples:

    - Online tax payment
    - Online license application
    - Online government forms
    - Digital public services


======================================================================
E-BUSINESS MODEL SUMMARY
======================================================================
      
      B2C:
          Business -> Consumer
      
      B2B:
          Business -> Business
      
      C2C:
          Consumer -> Consumer
      
      C2B:
          Consumer -> Business
      
      B2G:
          Business -> Government
      
      G2C:
          Government -> Citizen
      




MEMORY TRICK:

    B = Business
    C = Consumer
    G = Government
      
          FIRST LETTER = SELLER / PROVIDER
          SECOND LETTER = BUYER / RECEIVER

E-BUSINESS STRATEGIES
======================================================================

An E-business strategy is a plan for using digital technologies
to achieve business objectives.


IMPORTANT STRATEGIES:

    1. Online presence
    2. Digital marketing
    3. Customer personalization
    4. Mobile commerce
    5. Social media strategy
    6. Data-driven decision making
    7. Customer relationship management
    8. Omnichannel strategy
    9. Security and trust
    10. Continuous innovation


--------------------------------------------------
1. ONLINE PRESENCE
--------------------------------------------------

Business creates:

    - Website
    - Mobile application
    - Social media pages


Purpose:

    To reach customers online.


--------------------------------------------------
2. DIGITAL MARKETING
--------------------------------------------------

Use digital channels to promote products.


Examples:

    - Search engines
    - Social media
    - Email
    - Online advertisements
    - Content marketing


--------------------------------------------------
3. PERSONALIZATION
--------------------------------------------------

Business provides recommendations based on customer behavior.


Example:

    Customer frequently searches for laptops.

    System recommends:

        "You may also like these laptops."


--------------------------------------------------
4. MOBILE COMMERCE
--------------------------------------------------

Buying and selling through mobile devices.


Example:

    Customer purchases food using a mobile application.


--------------------------------------------------
5. SOCIAL MEDIA STRATEGY
--------------------------------------------------

Businesses use social media for:

    - Advertising
    - Customer engagement
    - Brand awareness
    - Feedback
    - Customer support


--------------------------------------------------
6. DATA-DRIVEN STRATEGY
--------------------------------------------------

Business uses customer and sales data to make decisions.


Example:

    Sales data shows:

    Product A = high demand
    Product B = low demand

    Business increases stock of Product A.

4.2 E-COMMERCE PLATFORMS AND TECHNOLOGIES
======================================================================

E-COMMERCE PLATFORM:
    A software system that allows businesses to sell products or
    services online.


A typical platform provides:

    - Product catalog
    - Shopping cart
    - Customer accounts
    - Payment processing
    - Order management
    - Inventory management
    - Delivery tracking





======================================================================
BASIC E-COMMERCE ARCHITECTURE
======================================================================

                 CUSTOMER
                    |
                    v
             Website / App
                    |
                    v
          E-Commerce Platform
             /      |       \
            /       |        \
       Product    Payment    Order
       Database   Gateway    System
                         |
                         v
                    Delivery





======================================================================
IMPORTANT E-COMMERCE TECHNOLOGIES
======================================================================

1. Web technologies
2. Mobile technologies
3. Database systems
4. Cloud computing
5. Payment gateways
6. APIs
7. AI and Machine Learning
8. Cybersecurity
9. Big Data
10. Blockchain

      
      ======================================================================
      1. WEB TECHNOLOGIES
      ======================================================================
      
      Websites use technologies such as:
      
          HTML
          CSS
          JavaScript
          Web servers
          Databases
      
      
      Example:
      
          Customer opens an online shopping website.
      
          Browser -> Web Server -> Database
      
          Database sends product information back to website.
      
      
      ======================================================================
      2. MOBILE TECHNOLOGY
      ======================================================================
      
      Mobile apps allow customers to:
      
          - Search products
          - Place orders
          - Make payments
          - Track delivery
      
      
      Example:
      
          Food delivery application.
      
      
      ======================================================================
      3. DATABASE
      ======================================================================
      
      Database stores:
      
          - Customer information
          - Product information
          - Orders
          - Payments
          - Inventory
      
      
      Example:
      
          Customer:
      
              ID = 101
              Name = Ram
      
          Order:
      
              Order ID = O1001
              Product = Laptop
              Price = Rs. 70,000
      
      
      ======================================================================
      4. CLOUD COMPUTING
      ======================================================================
      
      Cloud computing provides computing resources through the Internet.
      
      
      E-commerce companies can use cloud services for:
      
          - Storage
          - Servers
          - Databases
          - Backup
          - Scalability
      
      
      Example:
      
          During a festival sale:
      
          Normal traffic = 10,000 users
      
          Festival traffic = 100,000 users
      
          Cloud infrastructure can scale resources according to demand.
      
      
      ======================================================================
      5. PAYMENT GATEWAY
      ======================================================================
      
      A payment gateway securely processes online payments.
      
      
      Basic flow:
      
          Customer
             |
             v
          Checkout
             |
             v
          Payment Gateway
             |
             v
          Bank / Wallet
             |
             v
          Payment Confirmation
      
      
      Examples of payment methods:
      
          - Debit/Credit Card
          - Mobile Wallet
          - Internet Banking
          - QR Payment
      
      
      ======================================================================
      6. API
      ======================================================================
      
      API = Application Programming Interface
      
      
      API allows different software systems to communicate with each
      other.
      
      
      Example:
      
          E-commerce website
                 |
                 | API
                 v
          Payment Service
      
      
      The website sends payment information through an API and receives
      the payment status.
      
      
      ======================================================================
      7. AI AND MACHINE LEARNING
      ======================================================================
      
      AI can be used for:
      
          - Product recommendation
          - Chatbots
          - Fraud detection
          - Demand prediction
          - Personalized advertising
          - Customer behavior analysis
      
      
      Example:
      
          Customer buys a mobile phone.
      
          AI recommends:
      
              Mobile cover
              Screen protector
              Earphones
      
      
      ======================================================================
      8. CYBERSECURITY
      ======================================================================
      
      Cybersecurity protects:
      
          - Customer information
          - Payment information
          - Passwords
          - Business systems
      
      
      Common technologies:
      
          - Encryption
          - Authentication
          - Firewalls
          - Multi-factor authentication
          - Secure connections
      
      
      ======================================================================
      9. BIG DATA
      ======================================================================
      
      Big Data means very large and complex datasets that can be
      analyzed to identify useful patterns.
      
      
      E-commerce companies analyze:
      
          - Customer clicks
          - Purchases
          - Search history
          - Reviews
          - Product views
      
      
      Example:
      
          1 million customers search for smartphones.
      
          Business analyzes this data to predict future demand.
      
      
      ======================================================================
      10. BLOCKCHAIN
      ======================================================================
      
      Blockchain is a distributed digital ledger that records
      transactions in a secure and tamper-resistant manner.
      
      
      Possible applications:
      
          - Supply chain tracking
          - Product authenticity
          - Digital payments
          - Smart contracts

4.3 IMPACT OF E-COMMERCE ON TRADITIONAL BUSINESS MODELS
======================================================================

E-commerce has significantly changed how traditional businesses
operate.


TRADITIONAL BUSINESS:
---------------------------
    Physical Store
          |
          v
    Local Customers
          |
          v
    Cash/Card Payment





E-COMMERCE:
---------------------------
    Website / App
          |
          v
    Global Customers
          |
          v
    Online Payment
          |
          v
    Home Delivery



======================================================================
MAJOR IMPACTS
======================================================================

1. Wider Market Reach
2. 24/7 Business
3. Lower Operating Costs
4. Increased Competition
5. Customer Convenience
6. Personalization
7. Faster Transactions
8. New Business Models
9. Changes in Supply Chain
10. Data-driven decisions




--------------------------------------------------
1. WIDER MARKET REACH
--------------------------------------------------

Traditional business:

    Mostly local customers.


E-commerce:

    Can reach national and international customers.


Example:

    A small Nepali handicraft business can sell products to
    customers outside Nepal through an online platform.


--------------------------------------------------
2. 24/7 BUSINESS
--------------------------------------------------

Physical stores have opening and closing hours.

Online stores can accept orders:

    24 hours
    7 days a week



--------------------------------------------------
3. LOWER OPERATING COST
--------------------------------------------------

Online businesses may reduce costs related to:

    - Physical stores
    - Rent
    - Large showroom
    - Some staffing requirements


However:

    Technology, logistics, payment and marketing costs still exist.




--------------------------------------------------
4. INCREASED COMPETITION
--------------------------------------------------

Customers can easily compare:

    - Price
    - Quality
    - Reviews
    - Features
    - Delivery


Therefore businesses face greater competition.



--------------------------------------------------
5. CUSTOMER CONVENIENCE
--------------------------------------------------

Customers can:

    - Shop from home
    - Compare products
    - Order anytime
    - Track delivery
    - Pay digitally


--------------------------------------------------
6. PERSONALIZATION
--------------------------------------------------

Traditional stores:

    Same offers may be shown to many customers.


E-commerce:

    Offers can be personalized based on customer behavior.


--------------------------------------------------
7. FASTER TRANSACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------

Online:

    Search -> Select -> Pay -> Order


The complete purchase process can happen within minutes.


--------------------------------------------------
8. NEW BUSINESS MODELS
--------------------------------------------------

E-commerce has enabled:

    - Subscription services
    - Online marketplaces
    - Digital products
    - On-demand services
    - Platform businesses




======================================================================
POSITIVE AND NEGATIVE IMPACT
======================================================================

POSITIVE:

    + Global reach
    + 24/7 availability
    + Customer convenience
    + Lower physical-store costs
    + Easy price comparison
    + Personalized services
    + Faster transactions
    + Better data collection


NEGATIVE:

    - Cybersecurity risks
    - Privacy concerns
    - High competition
    - Delivery problems
    - Dependence on Internet
    - Digital fraud
    - Reduced need for some physical stores
    - Return/refund challenges








======================================================================
TRADITIONAL BUSINESS vs E-COMMERCE
======================================================================

Feature             Traditional       E-Commerce
---------------------------------------------------------------
Location            Physical store    Online
Time                Limited hours     24/7
Market              Mostly local      Global
Payment             Cash/Card         Digital/Online
Customer reach      Limited           Wider
Data collection     Limited           Extensive
Product comparison  Difficult         Easy
Delivery            Customer visits   Home delivery
Marketing           Offline           Digital
Operating cost      Often higher      Can be lower

4.4 FUTURE TECHNOLOGIES IN E-BUSINESS AND E-COMMERCE
======================================================================

Future e-business will increasingly depend on advanced digital
technologies.


IMPORTANT FUTURE TECHNOLOGIES:

    1. Artificial Intelligence
    2. Machine Learning
    3. Generative AI
    4. Internet of Things (IoT)
    5. Augmented Reality (AR)
    6. Virtual Reality (VR)
    7. Blockchain
    8. Big Data Analytics
    9. Cloud Computing
    10. Robotics and Automation
    11. Voice Commerce
    12. Digital Payments


======================================================================
1. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE
======================================================================

AI can automate decision-making and customer interaction.


Applications:

    - Chatbots
    - Product recommendations
    - Fraud detection
    - Customer support
    - Demand forecasting


Example:

    Customer asks:

        "Which laptop is good for programming?"

    AI chatbot provides suitable recommendations.


======================================================================
2. GENERATIVE AI
======================================================================

Generative AI can create:

    - Product descriptions
    - Advertisements
    - Marketing content
    - Images
    - Customer responses


Example:

    Business provides product information.

    Generative AI creates:

    "High-performance laptop with 16 GB RAM and
     fast SSD storage, suitable for programming..."


======================================================================
3. INTERNET OF THINGS (IoT)
======================================================================

IoT connects physical devices to the Internet.


E-commerce applications:

    - Smart inventory
    - Warehouse monitoring
    - Delivery tracking
    - Smart shelves
    - Automated ordering


Example:

    Warehouse sensor detects:

    Product stock < Minimum level

    System automatically creates a restocking request.


======================================================================
4. AUGMENTED REALITY (AR)
======================================================================

AR adds digital information to the real-world environment.


Example:

    Customer wants to buy furniture.

    AR application shows:

        "How will this sofa look in my room?"


Customer can view the product virtually before purchasing.


======================================================================
5. VIRTUAL REALITY (VR)
======================================================================

VR creates an immersive virtual environment.


Possible e-commerce application:

    Customer enters a virtual store using a VR device.


Example:

    Virtual showroom:

        Customer -> VR headset -> Virtual Store -> Product


======================================================================
6. BLOCKCHAIN
======================================================================

Blockchain may be used for:

    - Secure transactions
    - Supply chain transparency
    - Product authenticity
    - Smart contracts


Example:

    Customer buys an expensive product.

    Blockchain records product movement:

    Manufacturer
         ↓
    Distributor
         ↓
    Retailer
         ↓
    Customer


This can help verify product history.


======================================================================
7. BIG DATA ANALYTICS
======================================================================

Businesses will analyze huge amounts of customer data to predict:

    - Demand
    - Customer preferences
    - Buying patterns
    - Product trends


Example:

    Data shows that umbrella sales increase during rainy periods.

    Business increases inventory before the rainy season.


======================================================================
8. CLOUD COMPUTING
======================================================================

Future e-commerce systems will increasingly use cloud infrastructure
for:

    - Storage
    - Computing
    - AI
    - Databases
    - Backup
    - Scalability


======================================================================
9. ROBOTICS AND AUTOMATION
======================================================================

Robots can be used in:

    - Warehouses
    - Packaging
    - Sorting
    - Delivery


Example:

    Warehouse robot:

    Receive order
        ↓
    Find product
        ↓
    Pick product
        ↓
    Move to packaging


======================================================================
10. VOICE COMMERCE
======================================================================

Voice commerce allows customers to search or purchase products
using voice commands.


Example:

    Customer:

        "Order a wireless mouse."


Voice assistant:

        Searches -> Confirms -> Places order


======================================================================
11. DIGITAL PAYMENTS
======================================================================

Future payments may increasingly use:

    - Mobile wallets
    - QR codes
    - Contactless payments
    - Biometric authentication
    - Digital currencies


======================================================================
COMPLETE FUTURE E-COMMERCE EXAMPLE
======================================================================

Suppose a customer wants to buy a laptop.


STEP 1:
    AI recommends laptops based on customer requirements.


STEP 2:
    AR shows how the laptop/product setup may look in the
    customer's environment.


STEP 3:
    Customer places order through mobile application.


STEP 4:
    Payment gateway processes payment.


STEP 5:
    Cloud system processes the order.


STEP 6:
    IoT sensors update inventory.


STEP 7:
    Warehouse robot picks the product.


STEP 8:
    Delivery system tracks shipment.


STEP 9:
    Customer receives product.


STEP 10:
    AI analyzes customer feedback and future preferences.
